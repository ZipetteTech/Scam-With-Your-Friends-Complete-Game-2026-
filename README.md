## About Scam With Your Friends

**Scam With Your Friends** is a cooperative comedy simulation game developed by JakeHub and Metater.

Players take control of a fictional scam call center based in Kolkata, India, where they must work together to manipulate AI-powered callers, complete increasingly ridiculous scenarios, and generate enough money to satisfy their demanding boss.

The objective is simple: reach your daily financial quota, avoid getting caught, manage your resources, and survive the increasingly unpredictable environment of your workplace.

However, things can quickly become chaotic.

From unexpected conversations with AI-powered characters to computer viruses, security threats, workplace disasters, and competitive performance reviews, every workday introduces new challenges.

## Main Gameplay Features

### AI-Powered Conversations

One of the defining features of Scam With Your Friends is its dynamic artificial intelligence conversation system.

Players interact with fictional AI-generated callers whose responses can change depending on conversations, decisions, and actions.

This creates unpredictable situations where communication, improvisation, and teamwork become essential.

### Online Multiplayer & Co-op

Play alongside friends or join public multiplayer lobbies.

Coordinate strategies, divide responsibilities, communicate with teammates, and work together to achieve daily financial objectives.

The game also supports single-player gameplay.

### Scam Scenarios & Financial Objectives

Explore different fictional scam scenarios, each featuring its own mechanics, applications, objectives, and potential rewards.

Learn how the game's simulated systems work, including:

- Investment and cryptocurrency-related scenarios.
- Gift card operations.
- Credit card applications.
- Lottery mechanics.
- IP geolocation challenges.
- Money Mule missions.
- Caller manipulation and dialogue-based objectives.

### Computer Access & In-Game Applications

The game features a simulated computer environment containing various applications and interactive tools.

Players can interact with fictional computer systems, access simulated information, use applications, and manage different activities during their shifts.

### Workplace Chaos & Survival

Reaching your financial quota is only part of the challenge.

Players must also deal with unexpected events, including:

- Computer viruses.
- Scambaiters.
- Police raids.
- Workplace disasters.
- Unexpected interruptions.
- Performance reviews.

Every decision can influence the outcome of a workday.
